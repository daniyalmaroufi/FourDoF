# 4 DoF Testing – Free Space Tests & Drilling

### Glossary
*   **OTT:** Outer Tube Translation (mm)
*   **ITT:** Inner Tube Translation (mm)
*   **OTR:** Outer Tube Rotation (degrees)
*   **ITR:** Inner Tube Rotation (degrees)
*   **RoC:** Radius of Curvature (50 mm for all curved sections)
